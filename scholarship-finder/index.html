How it works

Tell Scholarship Finder about your study goal and constraints. It researches current opportunities and turns a large list into a practical shortlist.

1. Discover

Search for relevant Master's scholarships across countries, universities, governments, and scholarship providers.

2. Verify

Check funding, eligibility, deadlines, and official application information. Uncertain details are clearly flagged.

3. Prioritize

Rank opportunities as Strong Match, Possible Match, or Long Shot so you know where to focus first.

Built for realistic decisions

Scholarship Finder is designed to help applicants compare opportunities based on their actual profile, not just collect scholarship links. Always confirm final requirements and deadlines on the official provider website before applying.
