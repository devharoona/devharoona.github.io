Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Scholarship Finder is a scholarship research tool created by Haroon Rasool. This policy explains the practices of this public website and the Scholarship Finder plugin.

Website

This site is a simple informational website. It does not provide account registration, payment forms, or a scholarship application service.

Information used by Scholarship Finder

To produce relevant results, Scholarship Finder may use information you choose to provide in your ChatGPT conversation, such as your intended degree, field, academic profile, nationality, preferred countries, funding requirements, or exam constraints. It uses that information to tailor scholarship research.

Scholarship research

Scholarship Finder searches publicly available information and may direct you to university, government, or scholarship-provider websites. Those external websites have their own privacy practices, which you should review before submitting personal information.

Data and advertising

Scholarship Finder does not sell your information and this static website does not intentionally collect personal information through forms. Scholarship Finder is not a scholarship provider and does not process scholarship applications.

Questions

For support about using the tool, visit the Support page.
